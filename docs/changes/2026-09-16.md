# 2026-09-16 — Fix over-length meta descriptions on 5 page-1 posts (CTR fix)

## Root Cause

All 37 EN posts had meta descriptions authored **longer than the ~155-char SERP
display limit** (median 249, max 371). Google truncated every one mid-clause, so
each listing ended on an unfinished sentence — no completion, no CTA, no reason to
click.

This explains the anomaly that had been misdiagnosed as an *authority* problem:
four EN posts sit on **page 1** (positions 3.2 / 5.3 / 6.5 / 7.0) with **0 clicks
across 30 days**. A position-3 ranking proves Google finds the page relevant; a 0%
CTR at that position is a *snippet* failure, not a ranking failure. The prior
3 backlink attempts + 2 on-page tunes were aimed at the wrong layer — and
`no-code-vibe-code-real-code` (pos 70.9) never had impression volume to fix.

ZH was unaffected: 0 of 37 ZH posts exceed the limit. Google's CJK display limit is
~78 *characters* (2 display units each), and the Chinese descriptions are already
written within it.

## Changes

- Rewrote the `description:` frontmatter on the 5 page-1 / highest-value EN posts
- Rule applied: same intent + same hook, ≤155 chars, **complete sentence**, ending
  on a reason to click
- Titles, bodies, tags, dates and all ZH content untouched — this is a
  display-layer fix only
- No new content, no new posts, no cover changes
