## The spine — corrected against the queue

The queue's proposed spine was *"formation is easy, funding didn't follow."* The primary sources say
something sharper, so the post was written against the sources:

```
queue's framing   solo founders are only ~17% of VC-funded deals → can't raise
actual measurement  17% is a RATIO OF POPULATIONS, not a failure rate
                    Series A median valuation: solo $54.9M vs multi $53.6M  (within 2.5%)
                    the real gap is DISPERSION: top-decile ÷ median solo revenue
                                                     4 yrs ago 34x  →  2025  61x
                    bootstrapped, 99th pct, month 24: solo within 5% of multi
```

So the post's actual thesis: **the gate never closed — more people stopped walking through it**,
because forming alone got cheap enough that the formation pool now fills with companies that will
bootstrap. And the number that gets quoted backwards is named and corrected explicitly.

Primary sources, all verified at origin (not recalled):

```
Stripe Atlas blog      Q2-2026 Solo C-corp share 63% (all-time high); median 6-mo revenue −23% YoY
                       vs top decile +19%; dispersion 34x → 61x; the 5 separating traits
                       (AI-native ~2x, B2B ~30% more likely, 10 vs 3 countries month 1, 30% vs 8% retention)
Solo Founders Report   Series A medians $54.9M vs $53.6M; +75% personal ownership at exit
(Carta data)           top-decile multi at month 24 = +53% vs top-decile solo; 99th pct bootstrapped = within 5%
Eqvista (2026-08-18)   solo = 35% of new formations vs flat 17% of closed VC rounds
Carta (via 4WKMBA)     solo-founded share 23.7% (2019) → >⅓ of new US companies
```

⚠️ The **+53% multi-founder** figure and the **99th-percentile 5%** caveat are **both carried in the
post**, adjacent, with the distinction stated. The strongest counter-argument sits in the same dataset
as the thesis and omitting it would have made the piece a puff piece. Same discipline as the Sharp
funding disclosure on the traindecade whey page.
