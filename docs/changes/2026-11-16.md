## Cover — two drafts, first was REJECTED

Draft 1 prompt used metal plates + an engraver's chisel, and closed with
`no engraved words anywhere in the frame`. The model rendered **"MADE IN USA"** engraved into
the rusty plate.

This is the documented third failure mode in `decade-content-pipeline` →
`references/cover-generation.md`: **naming a forbidden object, even inside a negation, puts the
token in the prompt and raises its probability.** Writing "engraved words" produced engraved words.

Draft 2 fix was not a harder negation — it changed the object category to things with no
marking prior (two wet/dry river stones + a plain wooden stick) and dropped the word entirely.
Verified clean by enumeration: zero text, no people/hands/faces.
