## The spine (upgraded from the queue's framing)

The queue under-sold Carta's data. The interesting finding is a three-part gap:

```
solo founders hire FASTER than multi-founder (median 399d vs 480d)
solo founders hold ~2x founding equity (100% vs a 50/50 split)
BUT first-hire equity grants are ~IDENTICAL (~1.5% either way)
  -> solos sit on the one structural advantage they don't use
```

Paired with First Round's finding that **timing is not predictive** — what predicts a working
hire is a clear revenue line or committed pilots at hire time. That combination is an un-owned
angle; a generic "when to hire" guide loses to Business Insider / Gusto / foundra.
