## ⚠️ Pitfall hit during this session — future-dated `date` field

First build silently produced **no page** while exiting 0. Cause: the frontmatter carried
`date: 2026-11-13T10:00:00+08:00` but the build ran at 08:39 HKT. Hugo excludes
future-dated content by default, so the post was valid, on disk, and visible to
`hugo list all` — but never rendered.

**Tell:** `hugo list all | grep <slug>` shows the page, `hugo` exits 0, and yet
`public/posts/<slug>/` does not exist.

**Fix / rule:** always set `date` and `lastmod` to a time **at or before** the moment of
the build, or pass `--buildFuture`. Do not round a timestamp forward for tidiness.
