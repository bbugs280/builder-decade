## The numbers — all re-verified from primary sources this session

| Etsy Census | 70% call shop a business, 29% sole occupation, 89% business of one | 2024 Global Etsy Seller Census, restated in FY2025 10-K |

⚠️ **Two corrections against the earlier session's framing, made before writing:**

1. **Market share was overstated.** The draft said "~47% of the **global desktop laser**
   market". The filing's 47% is specifically **laser engravers and cutters by GMV**;
   the broader personal-creative-tools category is 37%. Post uses neither as a headline —
   the margin (56%) is the load-bearing fact and it IS the filing's own number.
2. **"~21 months" needed pinning.** The 21.8-month figure is computed at **under two boards
   per week** — the volume at which a shop's *total* monthly sales equal Etsy's $156 average.
   Stated explicitly in the post, and flagged as generous because $156 is sales, not profit.

New derived table added that the earlier framing lacked: the full ladder (35 / 15 / 5 / 2 /
<1 boards per week → 0.85 / 1.97 / 5.9 / 14.8 / 29.6 months) so the reader sees the vendor
published only the top two rows.
