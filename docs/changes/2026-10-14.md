## The receipts (what the page previously lacked)

Before this change the page asserted three numbers with no support — "one honest post
beats a hundred DMs", "ten to thirty real posts", "~2% response rate" — and its own
Sources section conceded the pattern was *"observed pattern, not measured effect."*

New measured spine, all primary/platform-report sourced (site evidence hierarchy):

| Datum | Value | Source class |
|---|---|---|
| First channel, under $1k | 30/68 communities vs 7/68 outreach | 290-founder analysis |
| First channel, past $10k | 4/9 outreach vs 2/9 communities | same |
| Consumer founders starting with outreach | **0, at any revenue level** | same |
| Platform/marketplace concentration | top 100 of ~3,200 actors ≈ 80% of usage | single-platform extract |
| Time to first payment | 20% within 30 days (vs 8% in 2020); median 38 → 34 days | Stripe Atlas 2025 |

⚠️ **Caveats carried explicitly, not buried.** The 290-founder dataset is
self-reported and Reddit-derived (Reddit overrepresented; the past-$10k group is n=9).
Both facts are stated in the body AND the Sources entry. The marketplace concentration
figure is labelled an anecdotal single-platform extract, not a benchmark. This is the
site's evidence-validation frame — the post argues a direction while refusing to
overclaim a magnitude.

⚠️ **Correction to the cron's proposed evidence.** The Stage-1 queue proposed the
"30/70 build-to-distribution split" and a "78 visitors, 2 signups" pattern sourced from
HN/IndieHackers *threads*. Neither was used — the site's standing rule is
*"NEVER aggregator listicles"* / gov + platform reports + primary studies only.
The 30/70 split was not traceable to a primary source and was dropped rather than
repeated as folklore.
