# 2026-10-01 — builderdecade: retitle 3 posts to out-position vendor SERPs

## Why
The monthly GA-review build-tester path (Stage 5) was previously blocked by a missing
`terminal` toolset on the cron job; after the fix, a live GSC pull returned real data for
the first time (44 pages, 166 impressions, 3 clicks / 30d). Cross-checking the three
highest-impression money pages against live SERPs showed they rank page-1 top-half but at
`ctr: 0` — and the posts above them are authority domains (Gusto, Mercury, TechCrunch,
Kinde) whose titles ours were duplicating rather than out-positioning.

```
EN  when-to-hire-your-first-contractor   20 impr / pos 4.2  → SERP owned by Gusto/Mercury (payroll/legal how-to)
EN  solo-saas-pricing                     9 impr / pos 4.2  → SERP owned by TechCrunch/Kinde (benchmark-led)
EN  seo-for-indie-hackers                 6 impr / pos 5.3  → SERP owned by SEO-audience giants
```

The prior snippet-length hypothesis was tested and rejected: these three posts' descriptions
were already ≤155 chars (114–147). The real gap is title differentiation — each was a me-too
of the incumbent's generic framing, giving the reader no reason to click *this* page.

## Changes (EN only — 3 posts)
1. **`when-to-hire-your-first-contractor`**
   - title: `When to Hire Your First Contractor (and When Not To)` → `When to Hire Your First Contractor as a Solo Founder`
   - description: receipts-based, names the vendor "payroll companies" angle
2. **`solo-saas-pricing`**
   - title: `How to Price a SaaS as a Solo Founder (A Decision Rule, Not a Guess)` → `How a Solo Founder Should Price Their SaaS (Without Benchmarks)`
   - description: leads with "no competitors to copy" differentiation
3. **`seo-for-indie-hackers`**
   - title: `SEO for Indie Hackers: Rank for the Query That Pays, Skip the Rest` → `SEO for Indie Hackers: One Query, One Page, No Content Team`
   - description: leads with "no content team" differentiator

## Not changed
- **ZH twins untouched** — their Traditional Chinese titles are already valid standalone
  framings; the EN side is where the SERP competition lives. (The ZH titles now semantically
  diverge from EN, but per the site convention ZH titles are not necessarily EN mirrors.)
- Body prose, tags, covers, `date` untouched. Only `title` + `description` in the 3 files.
