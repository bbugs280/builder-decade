## Fact-check (Stage 4b) — primary sources only
- **NIOSH (2025).** *Safe Desktop Vat Photopolymerization 3-D Printing.* CDC.
  Verified verbatim: uncured resin contains acrylates causing asthma or dermatitis; let printer
  air out before opening cover; curing generates inhalable particles/gases; sanding generates
  dust containing cured + uncured particles; use gloves rated against acrylates and IPA, change
  frequently; **once curing is complete, surface resin is usually fully cured and dermatitis
  risk is significantly reduced**.
- **Formlabs.** *How to Prime and Paint 3D Printed Parts.* Verified: flat neutral grey primer
  highlights imperfections before painting; FDM layer lines may need full-model sanding; fine
  (>600) grit rework after priming.

⚠️ **Scope discipline applied:** the sanding-grit ladder and paint-layer sequence are described
as standard workshop practice **as stated by the cited sources** — the post says in-line that
this is not a controlled comparison of outcomes. Only the resin-safety section is framed as a
hard requirement, because that is what NIOSH is.
