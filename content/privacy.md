# Privacy Policy

This page explains what data Builder Decade collects, why, and the choices you have. We keep it simple because we think privacy policies should be readable, not legalese.

## What we collect

- **Analytics.** We use Google Analytics (GA4) to understand which posts are read and how visitors find us. This collects aggregated, non-identifying data such as pages visited, approximate location (country/region), device type, and referral source.
- **Advertising.** We display ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

## What we do NOT collect

- We do not collect personal information for marketing, and we do not sell or share personal data with third parties for their own use.

## Cookies and advertising

Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also learn more about how Google uses data when you use partners' sites or apps at [policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Your choices

- **Opt out of personalised ads** via [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).
- **Opt out of analytics** with any standard browser extension that blocks tracking.
