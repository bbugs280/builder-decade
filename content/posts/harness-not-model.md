The AI model is out.

Not gone — *out*. Finished as a place to compete. Everyone still talks about the model as if it were the whole story: which one is smartest this week, whose benchmark just moved, who shipped the biggest one. None of that is the important thing anymore.

Here's what actually happened. Token prices fell roughly **600×** in six years. A model that cost $60 per million tokens in late 2021 now costs pennies. Open-weight models do for free most of what the flagship ones charged for. The model became a commodity — something you rent by the meter and swap out whenever a cheaper one is smart enough.

So if the model is a commodity, the model is not where the value is. The value moved one layer up.

The **harness** is the part you actually own.

The harness is everything that isn't the model. The tools. The context. The memory. The guardrails. The workflow. The loop that decides what the model sees, what it's allowed to do, and what happens to its output. The model reasons for a second. The harness is what keeps working all day.

Think of it this way: the model is the cable, the harness is the rig. You do the work with the rig, not the cable.

And here's the part most people miss — while the model got cheaper, the *harness* got more valuable, for the same reason any scarce layer next to an abundant one does. When reasoning is cheap and roughly interchangeable, the thing that separates a system that finishes the job from one that stalls is the scaffolding around the model, not the model itself. The same model, two different harnesses, can rank first or last on the exact same task.

That's the shift worth paying attention to in 2026. For years the question was *"which model?"* The question that actually predicts whether your build ships is *"which harness?"* — and whether you treat the harness as the craft, instead of the model.

For a solo builder this is roughly all good news. You were never going to win the model race. But the harness is a thing one person can build, own, and keep improving — and nobody can rent it away from you. You rent the brain. You build the rig.

If the model is out, the harness is in. That's the next thing to get good at.
